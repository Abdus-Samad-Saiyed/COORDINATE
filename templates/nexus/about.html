Built for NIT Srinagar Students

One Platform. All Campus Services.

Campus Nexus is a decentralized, privacy-preserving digital ecosystem connecting students across hostels, departments, and transit routes.

Verified Accountability

No anonymous trolls or unverified outsiders. Every account is authenticated against official institutional enrollment records to protect student safety and trust.

Zero Commercial Middlemen

Commercial rideshare platforms exploit students with surging commissions and high fares. Campus Nexus enables direct peer-to-peer taxi and cab sharing with transparent fare splits.

Modular Infrastructure

Built as an umbrella system. Launching with COORDINATE for cab shares today, designed so any student or research group can build and plug in new services.
